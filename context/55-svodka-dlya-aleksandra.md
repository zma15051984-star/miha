**2.3. Когда и чем подаётся встречное заявление?**

## 1. POSITION — FIVE LINES THAT MUST NOT CHANGE ANYWHERE

**1.1.** The Shareholders' Agreement dated **2 June 2025** was concluded

**1.3.** **There is no clause in the Agreement establishing an obligation to
incorporate a company.** There is only a qualifier inside **clause 2.1** —
the words "**upon the opening of companies as and when necessary**" in the
middle of a sentence about the purpose of the agreement. It is a
circumstance, not an obligation: no deadline, no responsible party, no
corporate form, no share capital, no name, no consequence of non-formation.

definitions section at all**.

**1.4.** **PT IMPRESS YOURSELF CORP is a separate legal entity** belonging to

lines of business** under its own `NIB`. Mr. Prokofyev is **neither a
shareholder nor a creditor** of it. The Agreement neither mentions nor binds

**1.5.** The subject matter of the Agreement under **clause 2.1** is
**exclusively the cleaning of motorcycle helmets**. Mr. Zavialov's obligation
under **clause 6.1** — transparent accounting and online access — **was

## 2. THREE QUESTIONS — ANSWERS NEEDED BEFORE SENDING

**2.1. Is this letter needed at all?**
As we understand it, a somasi is not required before a criminal report:
filing a report under **Pasal 23 UU 20/2025** is not conditional on anything.
Can the counter-report be filed without the letter? What does the letter

**2.2. Is `Restorative Justice` the goal of the plan?**
Counsel described four stages: `Penyelidikan → Gelar Perkara → Penyidikan →

agreed solution concerning the repayment of the funds". **No offer of

examination on 25 September 2026. This point would therefore make Mr.

Separately: **point 25** demands withdrawal of the police report **and, in
the same sentence**, a resolution of the money issue, while **points a–c**
promise criminal reports if they refuse. **Points 10 and 11** of the same
letter describe exactly this construction as improper when the other side
does it. **A written assessment is required: does this create a risk of a
counter-qualification under Articles 482/483 of Law No. 1 of 2023?**

**2.3. When and how is the counter-report filed?**
We need the specific articles, the named parties and the timeline. The report
is the objective; the letter is only preparation for it.
